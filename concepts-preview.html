Compare All 4 UI Concepts

Review the visual language, typography, and controls of each automotive experience before making your selection.

CONCEPT 01
1. Luxury Dark Theme
Obsidian and charcoal backgrounds accented with precision crimson carbon details. Features classical serif typography (Cinzel), high-status automotive branding, and centered luxury timekeeping.
  • Deep satin black & charcoal finish
  • Subtle crimson glowing indicators
  • Center prestige clock & date
  • Circular frosted glass transport controls
CONCEPT 02
2. Minimal Gallery
Clean, modern editorial layout with architectural typography (Space Grotesk). Features an interactive bottom filmstrip gallery with live thumbnails, understated headers, and unobstructed photographic focus.
  • Clean Scandinavian minimalism
  • Live interactive thumbnail strip
  • Bold editorial vehicle typography
  • Subtle frosted navigation pills
CONCEPT 03
3. Futuristic Dashboard
Inspired by high-tech digital cockpits and HUD displays. Features cyber cyan neon accents, live vehicle telemetry gauges (HP, acceleration, drive mode), and digital monospace typography (Orbitron).
  • Electric cyan neon accents & HUD grid
  • Live vehicle performance telemetry
  • Digital cockpit system status indicators
  • Angular high-tech navigation buttons
CONCEPT 04
4. Cinematic Experience
Puts 100% of the focus on breathtaking, full-bleed automotive photography. Anamorphic letterbox bars, warm gold camera metadata, and an ultra-compact floating transport pill that keeps the image center stage.
  • Pure 4K cinema presentation
  • Studio camera & lens metadata tags
  • Ultra-compact floating pill transport
  • Subtle warm gold luxury accents
